MY PROCESS

Before I Design The Interface, I Understand The User Experience

Great digital products don't begin with polished screens. They begin with a clear understanding of the problem, the people using the product and the goals behind it. I move from understanding → structure → design → refinement → delivery, creating experiences that are useful for users and practical for businesses.

01 / THE IDEA BEHIND THE PROCESS

Good Design Is A Series Of Decisions

Every screen has a reason to exist. Before choosing colours, typography or components, we ask what the user needs to accomplish, what information they need and what the business needs the experience to achieve. The process keeps those decisions connected from the first conversation to the final handoff.

02 / PROCESS OVERVIEW

Discover

We start by understanding the product, the users, the business goals and the problem you're trying to solve.

03 / DEFINE

Turn Information Into Direction

Once we understand the problem, we identify the most important user needs and define what the experience needs to accomplish.

04 / STRUCTURE

Figure Out How Everything Should Work

Before visual design begins, we organize the experience so users can move through the product naturally.

05 / DESIGN

Turn Structure Into An Interface

Once the experience is clear, we create the visual language of the product and translate the flows into polished interfaces.

06 / PROTOTYPE

Make The Experience Feel Real

Static screens only show part of the story. Interactive prototypes help us understand how the product behaves from one interaction to the next.

07 / TEST & REFINE

Does It Actually Make Sense?

Design isn't finished when the screens look good. We review the experience, identify friction and refine the areas that need improvement.

08 / DESIGN SYSTEM

A Good Interface Shouldn't Have to be Redesigned from Scratch Every Time

Once the core interface is established, reusable components and patterns help keep the product consistent as it grows.

09 / DEVELOPER HANDOFF

Design Shouldn't Stop At “Here's The Figma File”

The final stage is about making sure the design can move smoothly into development. We organize the Figma file, components, responsive behaviour and interaction states so developers have the context they need to build the experience accurately.
