Overview

Impact is an interactive learning platform that delivers bite-sized courses, gamified learning tracks, and real-time progress analytics. It helps users acquire practical skills and complete certifications in a flexible layout.

Personalized Paths

Adaptive course mapping based on individual student pace.

Gamified Learning

Quizzes, achievements, and rewards to sustain motivation.

Insightful Overview

Keep track of completed milestones and daily study streaks.

The Challenge

Traditional EdTech platforms often struggle with low student retention and high bounce rates due to dry interfaces, complex course structures, and lack of visual motivators.

  • Low daily student engagement
  • Complicated navigation for course materials
  • Outdated visual hierarchies
  • Inability to track learning progress intuitively

Our Solution

We structured a micro-learning interface prioritizing visuals, clear navigation, and gamified elements. High-contrast typography and interactive cards were utilized.

  • Micro-learning course layout
  • Personal progress dashboard
  • One-tap lesson navigation
  • Vibrant, premium educational UI
